# Live-Action Urban Prop Image Generation · Constraint Manual

---

## I. Prop Design Principles

> Props in live-action urban settings are not "modeled objects," but "used items"—they have been held by someone, placed on a desk, or kept in a pocket. The camera captures them in their current state.

1. **Objects as Narrative** — Every prop is a miniature narrative container. Lipstick marks on a coffee cup imply someone recently drank from it; wear on a phone case implies long-term use; creases in an umbrella imply repeated opening and closing.
2. **Signs of Use Over Pristine Condition** — Props in live-action urban settings must have evidence of "being used." Brand-new, just-unboxed, or showroom-grade props are strictly prohibited.
3. **Authentic Contemporary Chinese Urban Items** — Props must be items that actually exist in contemporary Chinese urban life: Alipay/WeChat Pay interfaces, domestic phone brands, Chinese packaging, Chinese-style takeout containers—rejecting generic, country-agnostic fictional items.
4. **Material as Reality** — Not materials rendered with PBR parameters, but the real material behavior captured by a camera: stainless steel reflections, ceramic glaze, light absorption by paper, injection molding textures of plastic.
5. **Single-View Still Life Photography** — Props are presented independently as a single still-life product photograph, not as a multi-angle model display, and not as a 2×2 grid.

## III. Single-View Still Life Photography Standards

> Live-action urban props only require a single still-life product photograph, not a multi-angle model display. The following are the complete standards for single-view photography.

### Composition

| Item | Photography Requirement |
|---|---|
| Prop Presentation | Prop centered or placed on the rule of thirds, displayed independently, fully in frame, no cropping |
| Background | Plain gray seamless background paper #B0B0B0 (matte), or pure white desk/countertop (light wood grain/cream white desk), chosen based on prop attributes. No extra objects, no decorations, no text |
| Lighting | Natural window light or single soft light. Window light entering at 45° side (bright, soft, with natural shadows), or softbox top + side fill (product photography-level even lighting). Soft light and shadow, clear direction, no hard shadows |
| Depth of Field | Shallow depth of field (f/2.8-f/5.6), prop subject sharp and clear, slight blur in foreground and background to highlight subject |
| Shadow | Natural contact shadow between the bottom of the prop and the supporting surface—real, not cut-out or floating. Shadow is soft, direction consistent with main light source |
| Aspect Ratio | Recommended 1:1 square or 4:3, suitable for single prop display |

### Prop State Guidelines

| Dimension | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Usage Marks | Must have visual evidence of "being used"—not brand new/unopened, not showroom condition |
| Chinese Identity | Chinese labels/Chinese brands/Chinese-style packaging/contemporary Chinese everyday—rejecting country-agnostic fictional items |
| Material Realism | Not rendered texture—real material behavior captured by camera (light reflection, diffusion, transmission) |
| Scale Sense | Suggest real size through material texture density and craftsmanship details, no reference objects needed |

## IV. Materials and Surfaces—Real Objects Under the Camera

> The following describes the surface behavior of everyday urban items under a camera, not rendering parameters.

| Material | Real Performance Under Camera | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Metal (Stainless Steel/Aluminum Alloy) | Visible brushed texture, sharp but not pure white edge highlights, reflection of soft surrounding environment colors, fingerprints and micro-scratches on surface | Metal brushed texture, natural environment reflection, surface micro-scratches and fingerprint marks, non-rendered sheen |
| Glass | Transparent but with slight inherent color (glass cross-section appears pale green), fingerprints or water stains on surface, light refraction on beveled edges | Natural glass transparency, real fingerprints/water stains on surface, edge light refraction, non-CG perfect transparency |
| Ceramic | Soft glaze reflection (not mirror-like), fine crazing/scratches on surface, usage wear on cup rim and bottom | Soft ceramic glaze reflection, fine usage scratches, natural cup bottom wear |
| Plastic | Visible injection molding texture/parting line, fine scratches on surface, diffuse reflection texture of matte plastic | Clear injection molding texture, naturally visible parting line, fine surface usage scratches |
| Paper | Visible fiber roughness, natural creases (not deliberate), slight ink bleed in printed text, wear on edges from flipping | Paper fiber texture, natural creases, realistic printed text texture, natural edge wear |
| Fabric (Cotton/Linen/Canvas) | Visible weave texture, pilling/fuzz on surface, creases and usage wrinkles, slight color unevenness | Fabric weave texture, natural pilling and fuzz, realistic post-use wrinkles |
| Leather | Natural grain/pores visible, usage marks at bending points, natural wear on edges, oxidation on hardware | Natural leather grain, bending usage marks, slight hardware oxidation, real usage patina |
| Wood | Natural wood grain, scratches/dents on surface, usage wear on paint finish, slight color change | Natural clear wood grain, surface usage dent marks, everyday paint wear |

## VI. Constraint Rules

### Mandatory

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Must be "single still-life photography"—single view, not multi-angle, not 2×2 grid, not model display |
| R2 | Must specify "plain gray background paper #B0B0B0" or "light wood grain desk/cream white countertop" etc. real surfaces, complex scenes prohibited |
| R3 | Must specify lighting logic—window light or softbox, clear light and shadow direction, natural shadows |
| R4 | Prop must have "usage marks"—not brand new, not showroom condition, not unopened |
| R5 | Prop must have contemporary Chinese urban identity—Chinese labels/Chinese brands/Chinese-style packaging |
| R6 | Must declare "physical product photography + not 3D render not CG" as core anchor |
| R7 | Material must be described as real performance under camera, not rendering parameters |

### Prohibited

| ID | Prohibition |
|---|---|
| X1 | Strictly prohibit all CG terms such as "3D render / 3D modeling / CG / UE engine / Blender / PBR material" |
| X2 | Strictly prohibit non-photographic media such as "2D hand-drawn / illustration / animation / anime" |
| X3 | Strictly prohibit "multi-angle / four views / 2×2 grid / orthographic view / turnaround / blueprint"—only single image |
| X4 | Strictly prohibit props that are "brand new unopened / showroom condition / no usage marks / perfect condition" |
| X5 | Strictly prohibit non-contemporary urban items such as "ancient style/ancient/xianxia/wuxia/Republican era/cyberpunk/sci-fi/Western fantasy/alternate world" |
| X6 | Strictly prohibit "fictional items without Chinese identity / all-English packaging / non-Chinese brands" |
| X7 | Strictly prohibit "appearance of person/hand/finger/limb/prosthetic" |
| X8 | Strictly prohibit states where "prop is held/gripped/worn/in use/interacting with person" |
| X9 | Strictly prohibit "floating/cut-out white background/no shadow/no supporting surface"—item must be placed on a real surface |
| X10 | Strictly prohibit "watermark / text / LOGO / signature / border / AI generation traces" |
