# Storyboard Prompts · Live-Action Urban · Style-Specific Techniques

---

## Scope

This Skill is exclusively for generating storyboard prompts in the **Live-Action Urban** style, adapting to live-action film genres such as urban drama, workplace growth, youthful healing, everyday life, and urban suspense.

---

## I. Emotion Before the Camera — Faces, Eyes, Micro-Expressions

> Emotions in live-action urban settings do not come from "cybernetic light effects" or "rendering parameters," but from the micro-expressions of real actors before the camera. The following mappings focus on how the camera captures real people.

| Emotional Input | Facial Description | Eye Description | Micro-Expression Supplement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capable / Steady | Sharp demeanor, natural facial lines without tension | Confident gaze, looking straight ahead without wavering | Straight lip line, slightly tucked chin, composed and restrained aura |
| Sorrowful / Melancholic | Drooping brows and eyes, relaxed facial muscles | Dull, unfocused gaze, looking out the window or at the ground | Corners of the mouth naturally drooping, expression wistful, not forced |
| Gentle / Healing | Relaxed brows and eyes, soft facial features | Warm, focused gaze, as if listening intently to the other person | Corners of the mouth slightly upturned, not a smile, but warmth |
| Stern / Guarded | Facial lines slightly tense, not exaggerated | Sharp but not excessive gaze, strong focus | Tightened chin, restrained expression, defensive body language |
| Surprised / Touched | Outer corners of eyes slightly raised, brows naturally lifted | Gaze suddenly focused, pupils slightly dilated | Mouth slightly open, expression vivid but not exaggerated |
| Contemplative / Introspective | Calm demeanor, relaxed muscles around the eyes | Gaze distant and unfocused, as if looking but not seeing | Calm expression, possibly resting chin on one hand or lightly tapping fingers |
| Joyful / Vivid | Natural smile lines at the corners of the eyes, eye shape slightly curved | Bright, luminous gaze, corners of the eyes drawn up with a smile | Corners of the mouth upturned, cheeks slightly flushed, expression lively but not exaggerated |
| Tired / Weary | Slightly sunken eye sockets, overall facial relaxation | Hazy gaze, eyelids slightly heavy | Drooping brows and eyes, possible dark circles, relaxed expression |
| Expectant / Aspiring | Brows and eyes slightly raised, restrained small-scale facial movement | Gaze bright, pupils with light | Corners of the mouth slightly upturned, suppressed smile on the face |
| Resolute / Firm | Clear facial lines, chin slightly raised | Gaze straight ahead, no avoidance or evasion | Chin slightly tucked or raised, expression bold but not aggressive |

## II. Light Before the Camera — Light Source Logic and Atmosphere

### 2.1 Natural Light Moments

| Moment | Light Source Behavior | Color Temperature & Tone | Spatial Atmosphere |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early Morning | Low-angle warm white sunlight entering the room diagonally through windows; outdoor street sunlight just clearing buildings, long shadows | 3500-4500K warm white, slightly cool air | Quiet and unawakened, crisp morning mist, everything not yet started |
| Morning | Sun rising, cold white and clear, sharp shadows; outdoor building surface materials clear; indoor window light area at maximum | 5000-6000K neutral to cool | Order established, daily operations, efficient and clear |
| Noon | Top light or near-top light, short and heavy shadows; strong outdoor material reflection; indoor window light entering from above | 5500-6500K cool tone | Sense of pause, midday lull, the peak of the daily cycle |
| Afternoon | Sun shifting west, warm tone gradually strengthening, slanted light passing through windows or trees creating long shadows and light spots | 4000-5000K gradually warming | Languid, time slowing down, the longest stretch of the day |
| Golden Hour | Extremely low-angle warm golden diffusion, shadows at their longest, all surfaces gilded with a golden edge | 2800-3500K extremely warm | Fleeting preciousness, the only light source for warm memory segments |
| Blue Hour | After the sun sets below the horizon, sky deep blue-purple, natural light extremely dark and cold, artificial lights just turning on | 8000-10000K sky + warm point artificial light | Transition, poetic, day has ended but night has not yet taken over |
| Late Night | Relying solely on artificial light sources—street lamps, desk lamps, screen light, car lights. Most areas sink into shadow | Warm sources 2800-3200K / Cool sources 4000-6500K | Loneliness or privacy, the world has gone quiet |

### 2.2 Emotional Lighting

| Emotional Tone | Light Recipe | Cinematography Constraints |
|---|---|---|
| Workplace Restraint | Window light cold white key light (5000-5500K), possibly accompanied by screen cold light for fill, shadows retaining detail | Moderate light ratio approx. 1:3, retaining spatial depth, glass partitions creating multi-layer reflections |
| Everyday Relaxation | Large area window light diffusion (4500-5000K), curtains softening, no hard shadows | Low contrast transparency, shallow depth of field highlighting the character |
| Warm Home | Warm-toned desk lamp/floor lamp side light (2800-3200K), window light as auxiliary (if daytime), soft shadows | Warm tone wrapping, single warm light source as primary, shadows warm with detail |
| Urban Life | Mixed light sources—street lamps warm (2800K) + shop cold white (4000K) + car lights sweeping by | Warm tone dominant, cold light locally opposing, handheld camera presence |
| Rainy Night Solitude | Wet ground reflecting street lamps (cold base 6000K environment + warm 2800K light spots), rain streaks on window glass scattering | Coexistence of warm and cold, single warm light source on the face, rest sinking into cold darkness |
| Suspense Tension | Hard side light, deep shadows, significantly increased light ratio, cold and warm light opposing (e.g., cold blue outside window + warm yellow indoors) | High light ratio 1:8 or more, hard light outlining contours, shadows deep but with detail |
| Youthful Healing | Abundant natural light (5000-5500K), large area diffusion, sky light + ground reflection fill | Low contrast high key, transparent and fresh, shallow depth of field softening background |
| Late Night Vulnerability | Single warm light source (desk lamp 2800-3200K or street lamp outside window) creating island-style lighting, one side of face lit, one side dark | Minimal light source, shadows rich with detail, light ratio can reach 1:8 |

## III. Scenes Before the Camera — Live-Action Texture of Real Locations

| Scene Type | Elements the Camera Must Capture |
|---|---|
| Office Building / Office | Multi-layer reflections and transmission of glass partitions, blind window stripe light and shadow, personal items scattered on desk surfaces, half-bottle of mineral water opened on the meeting table, screen cold light reflecting on face |
| Rental Apartment / Flat | Clear direction of natural light from floor-to-ceiling windows or small windows, sticker residue and nail holes on walls, blanket casually draped on sofa, remote control and half glass of water on coffee table, slight floor wear |
| Café / Milk Tea Shop | Bar counter warm light and coffee machine steam, window-side seats with the most precious light, usage marks on cups and trays, handprints on floor-to-ceiling glass, street view outside the window as another layer |
| Old City / Alley | Mottled walls and climbing moss, real urban texture of utility poles and overhead lines, warm yellow light of old-style street lamps, hanging laundry, street vendors and handwritten signs |
| City Street / Intersection | Crosswalks and traffic lights, street trees and street lamps, road markings and wear, shop windows and neon signs, bus stop signs and empty seats for waiting people |
| Convenience Store (Late Night) | Fluorescent tube cold white island-style lighting, sense of order in shelves, cold light and foggy glass of freezers, deep black street outside floor-to-ceiling window, warm light corner in staff area |
| Subway Station / Carriage | Fluorescent light cold white lighting, glowing advertising light boxes, reflections from screen doors, wear on carriage seats, flowing light and shadow in the tunnel |
| Rooftop / Roof | Panoramic city skyline, guardrails and ventilation pipes, wear on ground waterproofing layer, clotheslines and clutter, sky color temperature and cloud state |
| Hospital Corridor / Ward | Fluorescent light cold light in long corridor, floor reflections, cleanliness and coldness of white walls and handrails, natural light outside the window as the only warm color |
| Campus / Classroom | Arrangement and usage marks of desks and chairs, chalk residue on the blackboard, natural light from windows entering the classroom, depth perspective of the corridor |

## IV. Cinematography Grammar — Lens Selection and Movement

### 4.1 Focal Length Selection

| Focal Length | Narrative Character | Applicable Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 24-28mm Wide Angle | Environment surrounds the character, sense of spatial alienation, urban grandeur vs. individual smallness | Urban empty shots, rooftop overlook, lone individual in a crowd |
| 35mm Slight Wide | Subjective viewpoint, immersive documentary feel, coexistence of person and environment | Street walking, indoor dialogue, everyday following |
| 50mm Standard | Equivalent to human eye, objective neutral gaze, the most honest lens | Character portraits, two-person dialogue, emotional gaze |
| 85mm Medium-Long | Compressed space, bringing emotional distance closer, background blur highlighting character | Close-ups, seeing from across the street, voyeuristic perspective |
| 135mm+ Long | Isolating the character from the environment, extreme gaze or surveillance | Isolated individual in the city, distant view, narrative sense of distance |

### 4.2 Shot Size Selection

| Shot Size | Framing Range | Narrative Function |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme Close-Up | Eyes/lips/fingers/object details | Emotional explosion points, key information revelation, sensory focus |
| Close-Up | Top of head to above chest | Character emotion, dialogue reaction, facial micro-expressions |
| Medium Close-Up | Top of head to waist | Character interaction, hand gestures, body language in dialogue |
| Medium Shot | Top of head to around knees | Primary relationship between character and space, multiple people in frame |
| Full Shot | Full body of character + part of environment | Character's position in space, action route |
| Wide Shot / Empty Shot | Full view of space, character extremely small or no character | Spatial narrative, emotional buffer, metaphorical blank space |

### 4.3 Camera Movement

| Movement Method | Visual Psychology | Usage Scenario |
|---|---|---|
| Static | Calm observation, letting time speak for itself | Everyday scenes, dialogue, empty shot blank space |
| Handheld Slight Shake | Presence, breathing feel, subjective intervention | Emotional fluctuation, street walking, intimate following |
| Stabilizer Flow | Elegant urban roaming, poetic | Important scene transitions, character entrance, space display |
| Slow Push | Attention gradually focusing, emotion approaching | Dialogue heating up, truth approaching, emotional progression |
| Slow Pull | Pulling distance, revealing full picture, emotional detachment | Farewell, chapter closure, space display |
| Tracking Shot | Walking with the character, subjective accompaniment | City walking, tracking, everyday following |
| Pan | Line of sight transfer, spatial relationship explanation | Character gaze guidance, relationship between spaces |

## V. Fixed Style Anchoring Terms

**Cinematography Anchoring (Required):**

Live-action photography, live-action film still, contemporary Chinese urban, cinematic photography, natural and artificial light scheduling, realistic color science

**Character Texture (Required when character shots are included):**

Live-action realistic photography texture, real skin texture and pores visible, no skin smoothing or silicone face, natural hair texture with flyaways, visible fabric texture and wearing wrinkles, natural micro-expressions, realistic body language

**Scene Texture (Required when scene shots are included):**

Live-action urban scene photography, real building materials and signs of age (mottled walls/worn floors/glass fingerprints), clear and traceable light source direction, natural atmospheric perspective, not a model room or rendering

**Reference Image Consistency Anchoring (Required in Reference Image Mode):**

Maintain character styling, face, hairstyle, and clothing completely consistent with the reference image, maintain scene style consistent with the reference image, maintain unified light and color tone, all shots presented as a continuous photographic record of the same character

**Style Closing (Fixed):**

Live-action realistic photography, contemporary Chinese urban film texture, cinematic photography frame, not 3D, not rendering, not CG

**Image Quality Locking Terms:**

Mode A (Chinese) — Default:
Live-action photography high-definition quality, cinematic frame texture, no subtitles, no watermarks, no title overlays, no UI elements in the frame

Mode A (Chinese) — In-Frame Text Scenes (when the frame contains prop text such as signboards/street signs/screens/books):
Live-action photography high-definition quality, cinematic frame texture, no subtitles, no watermarks, no title overlays, no UI elements in the frame, text on in-scene props such as signboards, street signs, screens, and books is clearly legible

Mode B (English) — Default:
live-action cinematic photography, real human skin texture, contemporary urban China, cinematic color science, natural light photography, high detail, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI elements

Mode B (English) — In-Frame Text Scenes:
live-action cinematic photography, real human skin texture, contemporary urban China, cinematic color science, natural light photography, high detail, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI elements, legible text on in-scene props such as signboards, street signs, screens and books

## VI. Aesthetic Prohibitions

The following terms/styles must not appear in the output prompts:

- ❌ 3D rendering / 3D modeling / CG / UE engine / Blender / PBR materials / next-gen modeling / volumetric light / ambient occlusion / ray tracing
- ❌ 2D hand-drawn / illustration / animation / 2D style / manga / chibi / low-poly
- ❌ Photorealistic (English prohibits photorealistic / realistic photography—Live-Action Urban is "live-action cinematic photography," not "photorealistic rendering")
- ❌ Over-smoothing / plastic fake face / zero pores / silicone face / wax figure feel / AI-smooth feel
- ❌ Ancient style / period costume / Hanfu / xianxia / wuxia / Republican era / cyberpunk / sci-fi / western fantasy / medieval
- ❌ Anatomical errors / limb deformities / facial collapse / proportion imbalance
- ❌ High-saturation fluorescent colors / neon oversaturation / cheap filter feel
- ❌ Non-contemporary Chinese urban fictional settings / generic city without Chinese signage## VII. Complete Generation Example
