# Storyboard Table Guofeng Cyber 3D Constraints · Guofeng Cyber 3D · Technique Reference
---

## I. Positioning of the Storyboard Table
The storyboard table is the core tool for directors to translate a script into camera language. The following provides only the constraint specifications for the Guofeng Cyber 3D style at the storyboard table level. **The core principle is "Guofeng core first, Cyber form secondary." Whether in classical ancient-style scenes or modern urban scenes, the Eastern classical elegant aura and narrative logic must be upheld, and Cyber elements are prohibited from overshadowing the core**.

---

## II. Universal Core Principles for Dual Scenes (Mandatory for Ancient/Urban)
1.  **Iron Rule of Style Ratio**: In all shot frames, the proportion of Guofeng core elements (traditional colors, Eastern forms, classical aura, Chinese aesthetics) must be ≥70%, while the proportion of Cyber tech elements must be ≤30%. Pure Western-style Cyber or tech stacking without a core is prohibited.
2.  **Dual Scene Adaptation Logic**:
    - Classical Ancient-Style Scenes: Use traditional Chinese architecture/costumes/etiquette as the base. Cyber elements serve as narrative accents and must not disrupt the integrity of the ancient-style worldview.
    - Modern Urban Scenes: Use New Chinese aesthetics and Eastern traditional cores as the base. Cyber elements must fuse with the urban scene and must incorporate implementable Guofeng symbols (New Chinese forms, traditional colors, Eastern etiquette, Guofeng patterns). Pure urban Cyber without a Guofeng core is prohibited.
3.  **Unified Rhythm Rule**: Regardless of Ancient/Urban scenes, the entire film defaults to a slow, elegant Eastern rhythm. Only segments with strong narrative conflict may temporarily break this rhythm, and the specific duration and trigger logic must be clearly annotated.

---

## III. Lighting and Atmosphere (Fully Adapted for Ancient/Urban Dual Scenes)
- **Unified Lighting within the Same Scene** — No more than two lighting schemes should appear within a single scene, unless there is a clear narrative turn (e.g., natural light changes like day-to-night or clear-to-rain, or a switch between reality and digital illusion). Cyber-specific lighting (neon, holographic projections, self-illuminating electronic screens, glowing cybernetic components) must be integrated into the unified lighting scheme for the scene. In **Ancient-Style Scenes**, the main neon colors and electronic light color temperatures within the same scene must remain consistent with ancient light sources (lanterns, candlelight, skylight). In **Urban Scenes**, Cyber light sources within the same scene must have unified color temperatures with basic Chinese light sources (New Chinese chandeliers, Guofeng streetlights, building Chinese-style contour lights). Intrusion of miscellaneous colored lights without narrative logic is prohibited.
- **Emotional Color Palette Binding** — Each scene must be bound to at least one emotional scene, covering both dual scenes:
  - Ancient-Style Basic Types: Courtly Opulence / Landscape Imagery / Boudoir Gentleness / Wuxia Severity / Festival Joy / Moonlit Serenity
  - Guofeng Cyber Exclusive Types (Universal for Ancient/Urban): Cyber Jianghu Severity / Digital Illusion Psychedelia / Cyber Court Power Struggle / Wasteland Ancient Architecture Desolation / Neon Night Market Prosperity / Guofeng Urban Workplace / Urban Ancient Alley Mystery
  Maintain continuity of primary + secondary colors within the shot group. The color palette rule is mandatorily "Traditional Guofeng colors as the base." Core adaptation color references: Vermilion paired with Neon Cyan, Azurite Blue paired with Fluorescent Pink, Ink Black paired with Electronic Purple, Gilded Gold paired with Ice Blue. Loss of the Guofeng core is prohibited.
- **3D Rendering Lighting Standards** — When describing lighting, the direction and intensity of volumetric light, ambient occlusion layers, and depth-of-field blur range must be specified. PBR material reflection/refraction effects under different lighting conditions are key to visual texture. Dual-scene specific rendering standards:
  - Ancient-Style Scenes: PBR material parameters for ancient building wood structures/brick stone/silk/jade must be specified. Simultaneously annotate the semi-transparent volumetric light layers of holographic projections, color spill control of neon pipelines, and reflection effects of metal components.
  - Urban Scenes: PBR material parameters for New Chinese building glass curtain walls/concrete/metal panels must be specified. Simultaneously annotate light reflection of glass-etched Guofeng patterns, volumetric light scattering of Guofeng holographic advertisements, intensity and range of building Chinese-style contour lights, and glow radius of glowing components on cybernetics/wearable devices.
  All lighting descriptions must clarify the lighting fusion logic between Guofeng materials and Cyber materials to avoid disconnection between the two.
- **Warm/Cool Tones and Narrative Stage Matching** — Use cool-toned light (Indigo + Ink Black + Low-saturation Neon Cyan) for severe/melancholic/oppressive/mysterious segments. High-saturation warm tones like Vermilion/Gold are prohibited. Warm tones (Vermilion + Gold + Rouge + Low-saturation Neon Pink/Orange) are only used in opulent, gentle, or festive segments. Electronic glitch light and strobing are only usable for strong narrative conflict points (e.g., system crash, memory flashback), and duration and frequency must be annotated. **Abuse is prohibited in both Ancient/Urban scenes**.
- **Lighting Transitions are an Advanced Technique** — Universal transition standards for dual scenes; lighting change points must be annotated:
  - Ancient-Style Scenes: Window gauze diffuse gradient to moonlit clear glow = passage of time from day to night; Lantern warm light gradient to holographic projection cold light = switch from reality to digital illusion.
  - Urban Scenes: Chinese streetlight warm light gradient to office building neon cold light = scene switch from daily life to workplace; Ancient alley window lattice light shadow gradient to skyscraper glass curtain wall light shadow = narrative transition from traditional to modern.
  All lighting transitions must maintain a slow rhythm, matching the elegant Guofeng temperament.

## IV. Environmental Dynamics (Fully Adapted for Ancient/Urban Dual Scenes)
- **Environmental Dynamics Add Visual Breathing** — Arrange at least one shot with environmental dynamics every 3-4 shots to avoid the frame becoming "dead." **Mandatory Requirement for Dual Scenes**: Every 3-4 shots must simultaneously address the fusion expression of "Guofeng Core Dynamic Elements" + "Cyber Tech Dynamic Elements." Disconnection of single-scene elements is prohibited:
  - Ancient-Style Scene Adaptation: Petal falling synchronized with holographic particle scattering; Candle flame flickering synchronized with electronic screen micro-light flickering; Gauze curtain fluttering synchronized with holographic projection light flow; Wind through bamboo grove synchronized with neon pipeline breathing light effect in the forest.
  - Urban Scene Adaptation: New Chinese rain canopy canvas fluttering in the wind synchronized with neon light flow under the eaves; Commercial district holographic Guofeng calligraphy particle scattering synchronized with falling leaves in the air; Office building glass curtain wall Guofeng pattern light shadow changes synchronized with micro-movement of indoor Chinese potted plant branches; Subway tunnel Guofeng light box breathing light effect synchronized with wind effect from passing trains.
- **Guofeng Environmental Elements Priority** — The core of environmental dynamics must select elements within the Guofeng worldview, covering both dual scenes:
  - Ancient-Style Scene Compliant Elements: Falling flowers, flying fluff, light smoke, flowing water, wind through bamboo grove, candle flame flickering, gauze curtain fluttering, water ripples.
  - Urban Scene Compliant Elements: New Chinese component wind movement, Guofeng pattern holographic dynamics, Chinese potted plant branch swaying, Guofeng lantern/streetlight light shadow swaying, Chinese water feature flowing water, Xuan paper/silk material fluttering.
  Cyber dynamic elements are limited to compliant content within the Guofeng Cyber worldview: Holographic projection Guofeng pattern dynamics, Neon pipeline breathing light effects, Digital particle calligraphy/landscape form flow, Mechanical component slow motion, Cybernetic component micro-light flickering, Floating Guofeng building slight hovering sway; **Universal Taboo for Dual Scenes**: Street graffiti, pure Western billboards, modern cars without a Guofeng core, Western Cyber machinery, and other incongruous elements are prohibited.
- **3D Particles and Volumetric Effects** — Fully utilize 3D rendering advantages to achieve the fusion of volumetric effects between Guofeng and Cyber elements. Dual-scene adaptation standards:
  - Ancient-Style Scenes: Volumetric light scattering of mist, PBR ancient building reflection on water surface, physical falling trajectory of flowers, volumetric rendering of smoke, synchronized with nested Guofeng calligraphy holographic particles and holographic petal trailing light effects.
  - Urban Scenes: Volumetric light scattering of rain mist, Guofeng pattern PBR reflection on glass curtain walls, physical falling trajectory of leaves, volumetric rendering of holographic projections, synchronized with nested Guofeng landscape holographic particles and neon light effect Guofeng contour scattering.
  All particle effects must annotate quantity, volumetric density, and movement speed, adapting to the elegant slow rhythm of Guofeng. **High-speed, ruleless particle effects are prohibited in both dual scenes**.

## V. Ancient-Style Action Rhythm (Fully Adapted for Ancient/Urban Dual Scenes)
- **Ancient-Style Action Core Rhythm: Slow, Steady, Introverted** — All character actions default to slow speed. Rising, turning, and raising hands should be annotated as "slow." Cyber-specific actions (cybernetic expansion, holographic operation, mechanical component extension) also default to slow and elegant. **Only in strong narrative conflict segments such as fights or crises can fast actions be annotated, and the duration of fast actions must not exceed 30% of the single shot's duration**; **Urban Scene Supplementary Standard**: Even in daily commuting, workplace office, or urban social scenes, character actions must maintain the Eastern steady and slow temperament. Rapid small steps and large fast movements are prohibited. Maintain the introverted Guofeng temperament throughout, and prohibit exaggerated mechanical show-off actions.
- **Costume Dynamics** — The fluttering of Guofeng costumes is a natural dynamic asset. The drape and fluttering effects of cloth simulation in 3D rendering should be reflected in the frame description to increase the "alive" feel of the frame. Dual-scene adaptation standards:
  - Ancient-Style Scenes: Must annotate the drape and fluttering effects of silk/cotton linen/gauze fabrics in traditional ancient-style costumes (wide sleeves, skirt hems, pibo). Simultaneously annotate the linked dynamics of neon pipelines, glowing patterns, and mechanical accessories on the costumes.
  - Urban Scenes: Must annotate the drape and fluttering effects of fabrics in New Chinese costumes (modified qipao, New Chinese suits, Guofeng daily wear). Simultaneously annotate the linked dynamics of glowing pankou, mechanical cuffs, holographic patterns, and Guofeng accessories on the costumes.
  Universal Taboo for Dual Scenes: Cyber costume components must not disrupt the core form and Eastern aesthetics of Guofeng costumes.
- **Posture Constraints** — Universal posture standards for dual scenes; character behavior must conform to Eastern classical temperament: Steady and slow walking, restrained and introverted gestures, no modern body language (such as shrugging, spreading hands, leg shaking, large waving, exaggerated body swaying); **Urban Scene Supplementary Standard**: Cyber-related body actions (holographic panel operation, access card swiping, cybernetic adjustment, electronic device operation) must maintain restrained elegance. Operation actions must be slow, steady, and precise. Exaggerated large gestures and Western body language are prohibited. Even for cybernetic characters, body actions must fit the Guofeng classical aura, avoiding a cold and stiff mechanical feel.
- **3D Animation Texture** — Action transitions should be natural and smooth, reflecting the refinement of 3D animation. Avoid stiff posture switches; pay attention to natural joint arcs and center of gravity transfer. Dual-scene supplementary standards: Transitions between traditional body actions and Cyber mechanical actions must be natural and silky, with no stuttering or frame skipping. Cybernetic actions must match human center of gravity and joint movement logic. Holographic operation actions must have clear spatial positioning and feedback animations, balancing 3D animation realistic texture with Guofeng Eastern expressive aura.

## VI. Camera Movement and Transition Taboos (Fully Adapted for Ancient/Urban Dual Scenes)
- **Prohibition of Fast Camera Movement** — Whip pans, rapid pushes, handheld shake, high-speed fly-throughs, strobe jump cuts, and first-person fast running camera movements all conflict with the elegant temperament of Guofeng 3D. **Prohibited by default for the entire film in Ancient/Urban scenes**; only in strong narrative conflicts (explosions, system crashes, life-or-death fights) can extremely short-duration fast camera movements be used within a single shot, and the duration must not exceed 20% of the total shot duration. Prohibit damaging the Eastern elegant temperament for show-off purposes.
- **Prohibition of Fancy Transitions** — Wipes, rotations, venetian blinds, flash white hard cuts, glitch art garbled transitions without narrative logic, and zoom bounce transitions are all incompatible with this style. **Prohibited for the entire film in Ancient/Urban scenes**; only transitions that fit the Guofeng Cyber temperament are allowed: Lighting gradient transitions, element matching transitions (ancient building flying eaves matching Cyber flying eaves, jade pendant matching Guofeng access card, lantern matching Guofeng streetlight), spatial depth push/pull transitions, slow dissolve transitions. Transition rhythm must be slow, matching the overall Guofeng temperament.
- **Utilize 3D Spatial Camera Movement (Dual-Scene Adaptation Standards)** — The spatial depth of 3D scenes can be used for slow push/pull/orbit camera movements, but a steady and elegant rhythm must be maintained. Show-off style fly-through/flying camera movements are prohibited. Dual-scene specific camera movement guidelines:
  - Ancient-Style Scenes: Slow orbit camera movement around ancient building courtyards can be used to present the fusion of ancient-style architecture and Cyber elements. Slow push forward from the courtyard gate, gradually revealing Cyber-ized ancient-style interiors in the depth. Default camera movement speed ≤0.5m/s.
  - Urban Scenes: Slow push forward from the Chinese-style gate of an urban ancient alley, gradually revealing skyscrapers with implanted Guofeng elements and neon commercial districts in the depth. Slow orbit camera movement around New Chinese office buildings, simultaneously presenting the building's Chinese flying eaves and Cyber light shadows of the glass curtain walls. Camera movement throughout must serve narrative and emotional expression, not mere special effects display. Default push/pull/orbit speed ≤0.5m/s.
