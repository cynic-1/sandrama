# Storyboard Prompts · 3D Chinese-Style Cyber · Style-Specific Techniques

## Scope of Application
This Skill is exclusively for generating storyboard prompts in the **3D Chinese-Style Cyber (Neo-Chinese Cyberpunk)** style. The core is the organic fusion of "Chinese aesthetics as the soul, cyber sci-fi as the form," **perfectly adapting to two major creative directions**:
1.  Cyberization of traditional ancient-style scenes (palaces, gardens, wuxia, sects, and other native ancient settings)
2.  Chinese-Style Cyberization of modern urban scenes (commercial districts, office buildings, apartments, night markets, and other modern urban settings)
Rejects the stiff splicing of elements; upholds the core of Chinese aesthetics.

## Fixed Style Anchoring Terms (Dual Adaptation: Ancient + Urban · Must Be Included in All Outputs)

**3D Rendering Anchoring (Mandatory, must be prefixed in all outputs):**
3D rendering style, high-precision high-poly modeling, PBR physical materials, 3D Chinese-Style Cyber, Neo-Chinese Cyberpunk, Oriental sci-fi aesthetic, cinematic lighting, ray tracing, global illumination, 8K ultra HD

**Character Texture (Mandatory when including character shots, immediately following rendering anchoring):**
3D Chinese-Style Cyber character modeling, high-precision PBR textures, clear Neo-Chinese clothing textures, maximum detail on mechanical prosthetics/glowing runes, fine physical rendering of hair strands, realistic skin texture, transparent texture on neon accessories/holographic props, rich light and shadow layers, unified ancient/urban styling style

**Scene Texture (Mandatory when including scene shots, immediately following rendering anchoring):**
3D Chinese-Style Cyber scene rendering, organic fusion of Chinese aesthetic core and scene carrier, rich building/space details, realistic texture on holographic projections/neon light effects, accurate physical texture on metal/wood/stone/glass materials, depth-of-field blur, volumetric light, ambient occlusion, physical light effects on rain curtain/mist, unified ancient/urban scene style

**Consistency Anchoring (Mandatory for reference image mode, placed before the core logic at the end of the prompt):**
Maintain character styling, facial features, and clothing/prosthetic details completely consistent with the reference image, maintain the scene's Chinese-Style Cyber fusion style consistent with the reference image, maintain the lighting and color tone unified with the reference image, do not arbitrarily modify core settings

**Style Closing (Fixed, must be included in all outputs, placed before quality locking terms):**
3D Chinese-Style Cyber rendering, Oriental sci-fi aesthetic, Neo-Chinese Cyberpunk core, organic fusion of Chinese aesthetics and cyber elements, unified ancient/urban scene style, PBR physical materials, cinematic theatrical rendering

Mode B (English):
no photorealistic, no realistic photography, no low-poly, no rough modeling, no plastic texture, no harsh messy lines, no cartoon style, no anime style, no western cyberpunk, no Japanese cyberpunk, no pure sci-fi without Chinese elements, no modern western architecture without Chinese traditional core, no messy cluttered neon lights, no stiff splicing of Chinese and cyber elements, no distorted perspective, no wrong lighting, no color chaos, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI text

---## Aesthetic Prohibitions (Strictly Avoid During Generation · Dual-Scene Adapted Revision)

The following terms/styles must not appear in the output prompt:

- ❌ Photorealistic photography / photo-grade realism terms (e.g., photorealistic, realistic photography)
- ❌ Chaotic high-saturation fluorescent colors / disordered neon without Chinese color logic / cheap digital feel
- ❌ Pure Western cyberpunk / Japanese cyber / pure sci-fi elements without a Chinese aesthetic core
- ❌ Low-precision modeling / rough textures / plastic-like materials / materials without physical properties
- ❌ Cartoon / anime / 2D flat design styles
- ❌ Flat design / lack of 3D depth / perspective errors / lighting logic errors
- ❌ Pure Western modern architecture without a Chinese aesthetic core / pure mechanical architecture without Chinese structural elements / rigid juxtaposition of Chinese and cyber elements
- ❌ Hollow content that loses the Chinese aesthetic core and merely piles up cyber elements
- ❌ Pure Western modern clothing without Chinese design / pure sci-fi cyborgs without a Chinese aesthetic core
- ❌ Vulgar, chaotic, or disorderly visual elements that conflict with the Chinese aesthetic tone

> 💡 **Exception**: Modern 3D rendering techniques (such as ray tracing, volumetric lighting, global illumination, and physical materials) may be used normally; modern urban architecture with a Chinese aesthetic core, New Chinese-style clothing, and modern scene carriers may be used normally. The core must adhere to the fusion tone of "Chinese aesthetics as the soul, cyber sci-fi as the form."
