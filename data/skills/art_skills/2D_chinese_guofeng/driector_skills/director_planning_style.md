# Guofeng 2D Constraints · Guofeng 2D New Guochao · Technique Reference

---

## I. Color System and Visual Tone

- **Color Base** — The entire film uses the core color palette as its base: Moon White (C1 #E8EAF5), Blue-Green (C2 #4A9B8A), Vermilion Red (C3 #C93752), Indigo Blue (C4 #2B4C7E), Golden Yellow (C5 #D4AF37), Ink Black (C6 #1A1A2E), Rouge (C7 #A94A5F), Ochre (C8 #965E3E), Vine Yellow (C9 #F5E375), Grey-Purple (C10 #7B6C85). The overall color temperature is neutral (5000-5600K), with medium-to-high saturation (55-70%), presenting a full New Guochao palette with rich Eastern ancient charm.
- **Warm/Cold Narrative Contrast** — Warm colors (Vermilion Red C3, Golden Yellow C5, Rouge C7, Vine Yellow C9) serve as visual signals for positive emotions/luxury/celebration; cold colors (Indigo Blue C4, Ink Black C6, Blue-Green C2) serve as visual signals for conflict/severity/isolation. Warm/cold switches should synchronize with the story arc, not be mixed arbitrarily.
- **Warm/Cold Allocation Strategy** — Wuxia severe/sorrowful/isolated segments use cold tones (Indigo Blue + Ink Black + Blue-Green), prohibiting Vermilion Red/Golden Yellow warm light; Xianxia ethereal/girl's daily life segments use soft warm tones (Moon White + Rouge + Vine Yellow); Palace luxury/festival celebration segments use Vermilion Red + Golden Yellow highlights.
- **Palette-First Principle** — Segment planning must first bind to emotional scenes (Xianxia ethereal/Palace luxury/Wuxia severe/girl's daily life/moonlit poetic/festival celebration, etc.), then determine the main color + auxiliary color and lighting scheme, avoiding "the plot is right but the emotional color is wrong."
- **Prohibited Color Domains** — High-saturation fluorescent colors, neon colors, and strongly digital-feeling colors are incompatible with this style. Hue shift tolerance ±8°, saturation shift ±10%, lightness shift ±12%.

## II. Lighting Scheme System

- **Lighting as Narrative** — 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional segments. During the director's planning phase, the lighting tone direction should be determined at the segment level, not specified shot-by-shot.
- **Cel-Shading as Anchor** — All lighting schemes must maintain the cel-shading flat-fill texture. Shadows use darkened tones of the same color family; black hard shadows are prohibited. Japanese rendering techniques such as volumetric light and depth-of-field blur are allowed.
- **Light Source Angle** — Front-facing shots default to 38.5° oblique soft light (cel-shading flat-fill texture); side-facing shots can be adjusted appropriately.

| Lighting Scheme | Scheme Name | Color Tendency | Applicable Emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Soft Light Clarity | Moon White base + Rouge subtle warm skin light | Girl's daily life, warm sweetness, healing freshness |
| B | Xianxia Ethereal | Moon White + Blue-Green + Golden Yellow edge light | Xianxia ethereal, ethereal mood, stunning entrance |
| C | Candlelight Warm Shadow | Ochre warm dominant + Ink Black shadows | Nighttime ambiguity, intimate warming, gentle warmth |
| D | Moonlight Cold Glow | Indigo Blue + Moon White cold light | Moonlit poetic, longing solitude, quiet coldness |
| E | Window Gauze Translucence | Moon White base + side light dapples | Boudoir interior, study daily life, daytime tranquility |
| F | Palace Radiance | Vermilion Red + Golden Yellow highlights + Ink Black shadows | Palace luxury, grand celebration, formal solemnity |
| G | Cold Tone Severity | Indigo Blue + Ink Black + 38.5° side-shooting cold light, no warm colors | Wuxia duel, sorrowful isolation, tense atmosphere |

- **Warm/Cold Light Allocation** — Warm light (Ochre warm/Vermilion Red/Golden Yellow) applies to positive emotion/luxury segments; Wuxia severe/sorrowful segments always use cold light. The director can adjust warm/cold switching points according to narrative needs.
- **Atmosphere Direction Mapping** — The atmosphere direction of each scene should map to one of the above lighting schemes (A-G) to ensure visual consistency.

## III. Texture Direction

- **Cel-Shading Flat-Fill as Anchor** — The core of Guofeng 2D: delicate lines, cel-shading flat-fill, Japanese animation rendering, smooth color block gradients, prohibiting color block breaks/color overflow.
- **Delicate Brushwork + Cinematic Texture** — This is the anchor for visual texture. It is not realistic photography, nor 3D CG, but a fusion of 2D Japanese animation rendering and Guofeng aesthetics.
- **Material 2D-ification** — All costume, makeup, and prop materials must be presented with cel-shading flat-fill: silk sheen uses smooth color blocks, metal reflection uses cold gloss lines, jade luster uses inner-translucent color blocks. Prohibit "3D feel" and "realistic photography feel."
- **Skin Texture** — Pink-white base, cel-shading flat-fill, natural gloss, soft texture, inner-translucent feel, skin transparent glow. Prohibit matte/dead white/waxy feel/oiliness/overexposure.
- **2D Does Not Mean Flat** — Guofeng 2D New Guochao emphasizes adding spatial and cinematic feel through lighting layers, depth-of-field blur, and volumetric light on the basis of cel-shading flat-fill, rather than a flat, depthless cartoon style.

## IV. Guofeng 2D Scene Spatial Elements

Unique scene elements of the Guofeng worldview and their visual narrative functions:

- **Gauze Curtains/Screens/Door Frames/Carved Window Lattices** — Natural framing composition props, creating a "can't see through" sense of layering and spatial depth, presenting exquisite carved details with cel-shading lines.
- **Courtyards/Flowering Trees/Rain Veils/Blue-Green Landscapes** — Natural carriers for negative space composition; the scene is the emotion: full courtyard of blooming flowers = relief, sitting alone in the rain = isolation, falling leaves swirling = parting sorrow, misty clouds = ethereal Xianxia mood.
- **Candle Fire/Moonlight/Window Light/Lanterns** — Light source carriers in the Guofeng world: candlelight = warm/intimate (Scheme C), moonlight = cold/isolated (Scheme D), window light = daily/quiet (Scheme E), lanterns = celebration/marketplace (Scheme F).
- **Flying Eaves/Dougong/Vermilion Palace Walls/Stone Arch Bridges/Classical Furniture** — Architectural elements presented with New Guochao aesthetics, rich details, clear cel-shading lines, materials with natural wear/aged patina feel, prohibiting brand-new flawless "plastic feel."
- **Use Scene Empty Shots for Transitions Between Segments** — This style has rich scene assets (different time periods/weather/shot scale variants). It is recommended to use scene empty shots for emotional buffering in segment connections, avoiding hard cuts.
- **Use Visuals Rather Than Dialogue for Turning Points** — Prioritize visual means (lighting sudden changes, shot scale jump cuts, empty shot metaphors) rather than relying on dialogue for explanation.
- **Foreground/Midground/Background Layers** — All scenes must have foreground/midground/background, eliminating flatness. Outdoor scenes must include aerial perspective (distant mountains like ink).

## V. Ancient-Style Instruments and Environmental Sound

Sound element constraints under the ancient-style worldview:

### Instrument Selection

- **Xiao (Flute)** — Core instrument for desolate, isolated, and sorrowful segments; highly compatible with moonlit poetic/Xianxia ethereal atmospheres.
- **Erhu** — For emotionally agitated, grief-stricken, and longing segments; the crying tone of the string pulling suits emotional outbursts.
- **Suona** — For segments with intense emotional fluctuation (great sorrow/great joy, fate turning points, climaxes); use with caution, but once used, it is a nuclear bomb.
- **Guqin** — For opening tone-setting/stable segments/Xianxia mood; used in combination with Xiao.
- **Pipa** — Accent for tense, urgent segments; rhythm driver for Wuxia duel scenes, not dominant.
- String music underlay can increase cinematic feel but should not overshadow the main elements.

### Instrument Combination Strategy

| Emotional Stage | Instrument Combination |
|---|---|
| Stable/Opening/Ending | Guqin solo or Guqin + Xiao |
| Xianxia/Ethereal/Mood | Xiao + Guqin + light bells |
| Sorrow Deepening | Xiao + Erhu |
| Emotional Outburst/Fate Turning Point | Suona solo or Suona + Erhu |
| Tense/Urgent/Wuxia | Pipa accent + drum beats + string underlay |
| Girl's Daily Life/Sweetness | Guzheng + Bamboo Flute |
| Palace/Celebration/Grand | Bianzhong (Chime Bells) + Guzheng + string underlay |

### Ancient-Style Environmental Sound

- **Typical Environmental Sound Layers** — Cicada chirping/insect singing / babbling streams / wind through bamboo groves / marketplace hawking / night rain dripping on eaves / fabric friction / wind chimes lightly ringing / falling petals drifting / faint sound of firefly light points.
- **Mark 1-2 core environmental sounds for each scene** to assist subsequent sound design. The richer the environmental sound layers, the more immersive the Guofeng scene.
