# Storyboard Table Urban Romance Constraints · Mature Urban Romance 2D Animation · Technique Reference

---

## I. Storyboard Table Positioning

The storyboard table is the core tool for directors to translate scripts into camera language. The following provides only the constraint standards for the mature urban romance 2D animation style at the storyboard table level.

---

## II. Lighting and Atmosphere

- **Unified Lighting Within a Scene** — No more than two lighting schemes should appear within a single scene, unless there is a clear narrative shift (e.g., indoor light switch → deepening twilight outside the window, sunny day → sudden light rain)
- **Emotional Palette Binding** — Each scene must be bound to at least one emotional scenario (first meeting spark/ambiguous tension/guarding promise/separation misunderstanding/reunion relief/night banquet lantern festival), maintaining continuity of primary and secondary colors within the shot group
- **Light Source Angle Standards** — For frontal shots, the default light source is soft side-backlight or diffused light; for side shots, adjust according to the visual needs. When describing lighting, distinguish between natural light (window light/sunlight) and artificial light (lamp light/screen light)
- **Warm/Cool Tones Match Narrative Stages** — Separation/oppression/coldness segments strictly use cool-toned light (Cyan-Gray C6 + Light Blue C3); warm tones such as Amber Warm/Smoke Pink are prohibited. Warm tones (Amber Warm C7 + Smoke Pink C5) are used only in segments of emotional warming or rising ambiguity
- **Lighting Transitions are Advanced Techniques** — Gradual shift from natural light outside the window to warm indoor lighting = passage of time from day to night; shift from a bright office to a dim apartment = emotional turn. Mark lighting change points in the storyboard table

---

## III. Environmental Dynamics

- **Environmental Dynamics Add Visual Breathing** — Gently swaying curtains, rising steam from a coffee cup, raindrops sliding over glass windows, flickering city lights, falling leaves. Arrange at least one shot with environmental dynamics every 3-4 shots to avoid the visuals becoming "dead"
- **Priority for Modern Urban Environmental Elements** — Environmental dynamics should use elements within the modern urban worldview: gently swaying curtains, traffic light trails, rain streaks across glass, leaves drifting over streets, rising coffee steam, flickering streetlight halos. Ancient style/fantasy elements are prohibited
- **Cel-Shaded Dynamic Expression** — Environmental dynamics should conform to 2D animation expression methods: hand-drawn texture of dappled light and shadow, line beauty of fluttering fabric, cel-shaded rendering effects of rain streaks and lights

---

## IV. Urban Character Action Rhythm

- **Restrained Actions** — Actions of mature urban romance characters should be introverted and subtle, with emotions not displayed outwardly. Key actions (turning around, raising a hand, looking back) should be marked as "slow" or "composed"
- **Clothing Dynamics** — Micro-dynamics of modern urban clothing (suit hems, skirt hems, scarves, coat hems) are natural visual assets. Slight fluttering of clothing with wind or movement can be added to the visual description to increase the "alive" feel of the frame
- **Demeanor Constraints** — The demeanor of urban romance characters should reflect a mature elite temperament: upright and composed posture, elegant and restrained gestures, no exaggerated 2D-style body language (such as exaggerated gestures, chibi-style deformed expressions)
- **Micro-Expressions Priority** — Convey emotions through facial micro-expressions (mouth corner curvature, slight eyebrow movement, eye shifts) rather than relying on large-scale body movements

---

## V. Camera Movement Prohibitions

- **Prohibit Fast Camera Movements** — Whip pans, rapid pushes, and handheld shake conflict with the cool and elegant temperament of mature urban romance 2D animation
- **Prohibit Fancy Transitions** — Wipes, rotations, venetian blinds, and flash whites are incompatible with this style
- **Utilize Depth of Field and Space** — The spatial depth of modern urban scenes can be used for slow push/pull or follow shots. Shallow depth of field can be used to create a cinematic feel, but a steady and restrained rhythm must be maintained
