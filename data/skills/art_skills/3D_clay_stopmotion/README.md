# Stop-Motion Clay Style Guide

This style is specifically designed for the "stop-motion clay texture" genre. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Stop-Motion Worldview**: Set against a retro/fantasy lifestyle backdrop, encompassing wooden houses, forests, streets, cafés, gardens, etc., reflecting the distinctive life-like atmosphere of stop-motion animation.
- **Clay Texture**: All characters, scenes, and props adhere to the standard of stop-motion clay animation, emphasizing the clarity of clay texture, the visibility of finger impressions, and the granular feel of the material.
- **Healing Nostalgic Aesthetics**: Building upon the clay texture, it pursues a cinematic atmosphere. The color palette is warm and soft, details are rich, and the overall mood is warm, healing, and nostalgically moving.
- **Emotional Expression**: Suitable for showcasing gentle, healing, and romantic stop-motion stories, emphasizing emotional expression through a handmade quality.

## Scope of Application

- AI art generation for stop-motion clay subjects (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual creations requiring extreme clay texture, delicate lighting and shadow, and a warm, healing atmosphere

## Prohibited Content

- Modern 3D animation styles, smooth plastic, CG rendering
- Modern elements, modern architecture, modern clothing
- High-saturation fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Dark violence, adult-oriented content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with refined features and a gentle or bright demeanor, with costumes and styling in the stop-motion clay animation style
- Scenes with rich spatial layers, clear clay textures, and an atmosphere that is both realistic and poetic
- Props, everyday items, and environmental details in retro/fantasy designs, with delicate handmade textures
- Storyboards and visuals based on stop-motion animation, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style is applicable only to "stop-motion clay" related projects. If other styles (such as modern 3D, CG, illustration, or modern themes) are required, please do not use this set of prompts.
