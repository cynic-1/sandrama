# Live-Action Ancient-Style Realism Style Guide

This style is specifically designed for "live-action ancient-style realism" subjects. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Ancient-Style Worldview**: Set in ancient China, encompassing residences, palaces, artifacts, costumes, and weapons, with no modern elements.
- **Live-Action Realism**: All characters, scenes, and props adhere to live-action realistic photography standards, emphasizing extreme detail and authentic texture in facial features, skin, hair strands, and materials.
- **Ancient-Style Realistic Documentary**: Pursuing a cinematic atmosphere on a realistic foundation, with strong light-and-shadow contrast, rich details, and an overall mood that is cold yet warm, sparse yet dense.
- **Sweet Romance Atmosphere**: Suited for depicting gentle, healing, and romantic ancient-style love stories, emphasizing emotional expression under Eastern aesthetics.

## Scope of Application

- AI art generation for live-action ancient-style subjects (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual creations requiring extreme realism, delicate textures, and an Eastern classical atmosphere

## Prohibited Content

- Cartoon, anime, illustration, or 2D styles
- Modern elements, modern clothing, or modern scenes
- High-saturation fluorescent or neon colors
- Suggestive, revealing, or see-through content deemed inappropriate

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with refined features and a cool or gentle temperament, with ancient-style realistic costumes, makeup, and hairstyles
- Scenes with rich spatial layers, ultra-clear material textures, and a realistic yet poetic atmosphere
- Props, weapons, and accessories with ancient-style designs, exquisite craftsmanship, and extreme texture quality
- Storyboards and videos based on live-action realism, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style is applicable only to "live-action ancient-style realism" related projects. Do not use this set of prompts for other styles (such as illustration, anime, or modern).
